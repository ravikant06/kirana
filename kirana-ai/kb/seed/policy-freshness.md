# Freshness Guarantee

Every perishable item Kirana delivers has a minimum remaining shelf life at the
time of delivery.

## Minimum shelf life at delivery

| Product | Minimum remaining shelf life |
|---|---|
| Milk and curd | 2 days |
| Bread and bakery | 2 days |
| Paneer and fresh cheese | 3 days |
| Eggs | 7 days |
| Packaged snacks and staples | 30 days or one third of total shelf life, whichever is less |

If an item arrives with less than this, report it within 24 hours of delivery
and we refund the full item price. You do not need to return it.

## Cold chain

Chilled items travel in insulated bags with ice packs and are delivered within
2 hours of leaving the store. Frozen items are kept at or below -18 °C until
dispatch.

## Organic produce

Products labelled organic come from farms certified under the National
Programme for Organic Production (NPOP). The certificate number of the supplier
is printed on the product page.
