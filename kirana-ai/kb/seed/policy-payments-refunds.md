# Payments and Refunds Policy

## Payment methods

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, Kirana wallet balance and
cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹2,000.
Payments are processed by our payment partner; Kirana never stores your full
card number.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds always go back to the original payment method, unless you choose store
credit.

| Payment method | Refund time after approval |
|---|---|
| Kirana wallet or store credit | Instant |
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Debit or credit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash on delivery | Kirana wallet, instant; or bank transfer in 5 business days |

Store credit never expires and can be used on any order.

## Failed payments

If money leaves your account but the order shows **Failed**, you do not need to
do anything. The amount is reversed automatically by your bank within 5
business days. If it has not arrived after 7 business days, contact support
with your transaction reference.

## Double charges

If you were charged twice for one order, the extra charge is refunded in full
within 3 business days of your report. Please do not place the order again
while a payment is still showing as pending.

## Price changes

The price you pay is the price shown at checkout. If a price drops after you
order, we do not refund the difference. If an item is charged at a higher price
than shown at checkout, we refund the difference as store credit.
