# Returns Policy

Kirana wants every order to arrive the way you expected. This policy explains
what you can return, how long you have, and how the refund reaches you.

## Return windows

| Category | Return window | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh produce (fruit, vegetables, herbs) | 48 hours from delivery | Report with a photo; no need to send it back |
| Dairy, eggs and bakery | 24 hours from delivery | Unopened, or spoiled before its use-by date |
| Packaged staples (rice, atta, dal, oil, spices) | 7 days from delivery | Seal unbroken |
| Household and personal care | 7 days from delivery | Unused, in original packaging |
| Frozen food and ice cream | Not returnable | Report damage at the door only |

## Opened packaged goods

Opened packaged staples cannot be returned for a change of mind. If an opened
pack is defective (insects, foul smell, foreign object, wrong product inside),
report it within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the product and the batch
number printed on the pack. We refund the full item price.

## Fresh produce

If fruit or vegetables arrive bruised, overripe or short in weight, report it
in the app within 48 hours of delivery. Attach one photo. You do not need to
keep or hand back the produce. A weight shortfall is refunded pro rata, based
on the weight printed on the delivery label.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Frozen food and ice cream, once the delivery has been accepted
- Items bought in a flash sale, unless they are defective
- Gift cards and store credit
- Loose items weighed at your request, such as cut fruit

## How to start a return

1. Open **Orders**, pick the order, and choose **Report a problem**.
2. Select the item and the reason, and attach a photo.
3. Our team reviews the request within 24 hours.

Approved returns are refunded as described in the Payments and Refunds policy.
Packaged items are collected by the delivery partner on your next delivery, or
within 3 days, whichever is sooner.
