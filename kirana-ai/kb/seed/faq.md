# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need an account to shop?

You can browse without an account, but you need one to place an order, so that
we can show you your order history and send delivery updates.

## Is there a minimum order value?

No. Orders below ₹149 carry a small-cart fee of ₹20. See the Delivery Policy
for delivery fees.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Not in the app. If the order is still in **Created**, cancel it and order again
with the new address. Otherwise contact support, and we will try to help if the
new address is within 2 km of the original one.

## Can I add items to an order I already placed?

No. Place a second order; if it is for the same slot, we deliver both together
and you pay the delivery fee only once.

## How does a flash sale work?

A flash sale offers a limited number of units at a reduced price. Units are
reserved in the order people check out, and the sale ends as soon as they are
gone. There is a limit of 2 units per customer per flash-sale product.

## How do I contact support?

Use chat in the app, email support@kirana.example, or call 1800-000-0000 from
8 am to 10 pm, every day. Support can also help with anything this assistant
cannot answer.

## Do you sell alcohol or tobacco?

No. Kirana does not sell alcohol, tobacco or medicines that need a
prescription.
