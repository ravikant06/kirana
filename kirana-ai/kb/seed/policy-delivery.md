# Delivery Policy

## Service area

Kirana delivers within 8 km of our store. Enter your pincode on the home page
to check whether your address is covered.

## Delivery slots and fees

| Slot type | When | Delivery fee |
|---|---|---|
| Express | Within 30 minutes, 7 am to 10 pm | ₹49 |
| Standard | Any 2-hour slot, same or next day | ₹25, free on orders of ₹499 or more |
| Early morning | 6 am to 8 am, next day | ₹15, free on orders of ₹299 or more |

Express delivery is limited to 15 items per order. A small-cart fee of ₹20
applies to any order below ₹149, on every slot type.

## Missed deliveries

If nobody is available to receive the order, the delivery partner waits
10 minutes and tries to call you twice. After that the order returns to the
store. We reattempt once in the next available standard slot at no extra
charge. If the second attempt also fails, the order is cancelled and refunded,
minus the delivery fee.

## Late deliveries

If an express order arrives more than 15 minutes late, the express fee is
refunded automatically as store credit within 24 hours.

## Contactless delivery

Choose "Leave at door" at checkout. The delivery partner sends a photo of the
order at your door. Chilled and frozen items are never left unattended; for
those the partner will call you.
