# Order Cancellation Policy

## Before the order is packed

You can cancel an order free of charge while its status is **Created**, which
is until our store starts packing it. Cancel from the order page in the app.
The full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

## After packing has started

Once an order is being packed or is out for delivery, it cannot be cancelled
in the app. You can refuse the delivery at the door instead. Refused deliveries
are refunded, minus a restocking fee of ₹30 for orders that contain chilled or
frozen items, because those cannot be resold.

## Partial cancellation

You cannot remove a single item from an order that has been placed. Cancel the
whole order while it is still in **Created**, then place a new one.

## Flash-sale orders

Flash-sale orders can be cancelled while in **Created** like any other order.
The units go back into the sale, but the sale price is not held for you: if
you order again, you pay the price at that time.

## Cancellations by Kirana

We may cancel an order when an item is out of stock at packing time, when the
delivery address is outside our service area, or when a payment fails. You are
notified by SMS and email, and any amount already charged is refunded in full.
